## Overview

UIU operates **three dedicated shuttle services** that run independently of the registered student bus routes. Unlike the registered buses, these shuttles serve as frequent, open back-and-forth connectors for students traveling through nearby or high-traffic pickup zones. No semester registration is required to use the shuttle services.

---

## Shuttle 2 — Kuril BRTC ↔ UIU

**Type:** Continuous back-and-forth shuttle  
**Introduced:** 21 September 2024  
**Status:** Ongoing

### Route
> UIU → Bashundhara City Gold Refinery Gate → 300 Ft Road → Kuril BRTC Bus Stand → and back

### Operating Hours
**7:30 AM to 5:00 PM (continuous)**

### Why It Was Introduced
This shuttle was created to relieve traffic congestion on the Notun Bazar–Saidnagar road for students traveling beyond Notun Bazar. It runs continuously throughout the day with multiple round trips, offering a quicker alternative for students coming from Bashundhara, 300 Ft, and Kuril Flyover areas.

> No fixed timetable — the shuttle makes continuous round trips between UIU and Kuril BRTC Bus Stand throughout operating hours.

## Shuttle 3 — EWU (Aftabnagar) ↔ UIU

**Type:** Fixed-trip shuttle  
**Announced:** 10 September 2025  
**Commenced:** 4 October 2025  
**Fleet:** 3 dedicated shuttle buses  
**Status:** Ongoing

### Route
> East West University Gate → Lohar Bridge → Bottola → UIU (and back)

### Why It Was Introduced
Introduced to reduce daily commute difficulties for students from Shantinagar, Malibagh, Rampura, and Badda who faced traffic congestion and safety risks on the dilapidated Natun Bazar Road.

## Special Event Shuttles

On major events such as **Convocation**, UIU deploys additional temporary shuttle services from multiple pickup points. For example, during the **8th Convocation 2025** (26–27 December 2025), special shuttles ran from Natun Bazar, Kuril, and Aftabnagar exclusively for registered graduating students.

These are not permanent services — schedules are announced via the [UIU Notice Board](https://www.uiu.ac.bd/notice/) closer to each event.

---

## Service Suspension Policy

All shuttle services are suspended during:

- Trimester / semester **breaks**
- Officially declared **class-suspension days**
- Government **holidays**
- Special events where campus access is restricted

**Recent example:** All shuttle services were suspended from **10 March to 26 March 2026** when classes shifted online.

## Upcoming Feature

An **app-based real-time tracking system** for all UIU shuttle services has been announced and is in development. Once launched, students will be able to track shuttle locations and timings live from their phones.
